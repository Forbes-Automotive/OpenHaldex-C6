Controller Options

Sends live data to can2gauge gauges over WiFi. With control allowed, a gauge can change mode and generation and run Learn (car stopped).

On a full lock request, over-reports engine torque so the pump runs to the relief valve. Maximum clamping force, but Returned % reads lower (80s). Higher pump load and current.

Polls the Haldex for live data (Gen5: UDS, Gen2/4: KWP2000). Needs Haldex CAN.

Uses the diagnostic channel. Turn off when using VCDS.

Sleeps after 5 min with no WiFi clients and both CAN buses quiet. Wakes on CAN traffic; the first frame may be missed.

Also puts the CAN transceivers in standby, drops the CPU to 10 MHz and lowers WiFi power while asleep.

Keeps WiFi on for bench testing with no car connected. Switches itself off as soon as CAN traffic is seen.
